1. Periodic Implementation Forums

Virtual meetings will include:

  • Regional implementation updates
  • Challenges
  • Emerging opportunities
  • Policy developments
  • New funding sources
  • Guest experts
  • Faculty insights
  • Cross-regional collaboration opportunities

Each meeting should feature two or three regional case studies highlighting successful implementation.

Every six months, the virtual meetings will feature a concise implementation update including:

  • Accomplishments
  • Milestones reached
  • Projects launched
  • Partners added
  • Obstacles encountered
  • Next priorities
