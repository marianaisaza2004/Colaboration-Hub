Academy Fellows Nominations

Nominate Fellows for the next Academy to keep the leadership pipeline going.

To ensure continuity of work and expansion of each region's Competitiveness Action Plan, Fellows are invited to nominate Fellows for the next Academy. Criteria for nominees can be found here. The nomination form can be found here.

Your nominations help build momentum for your regions. Moving forward, the annual Academy will open with a "showcase" in which new delegations present updates on the accomplishments of the prior year's delegation and as a basis for new work.

This opening Academy event creates continuity between Academy classes and a self-renewing leadership pipeline rather than starting from scratch each year.
